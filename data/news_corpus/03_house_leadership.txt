House Republicans prepare for minority leadership election

Republicans in the House of Representatives are choosing a new leader after the party lost its majority in the 2026 midterm election. The contest comes as members try to understand a defeat that reduced the conference and transferred control of the chamber to Democrats. The winner will lead the opposition during the final two years of President Donald Trump's term.

The leadership race has also become an argument over political style. Some lawmakers want a more confrontational approach toward the incoming Democratic majority, while others emphasize rebuilding the party's organization and winning back voters who focused on household costs. Candidates are telling colleagues that the party must do more than criticize Democrats and must present an affirmative program.

The decision will shape committee strategy, negotiations with the majority, and the Republican response to Democratic investigations. Democrats plan to hold their own leadership elections separately before the new Congress begins in January.
